# Beta notice

Ropework is beta software. It is given to you for testing, **as it is, without any warranty**. It may have mistakes. Do not rely on it for work you cannot afford to lose; keep your normal backups and the server's own history.

- **What it collects:** nothing. There is no telemetry. If you press Send feedback, the app builds a file you can read, and you decide whether to send it.
- **How feedback is used:** only to find and fix problems and to decide what to build next. We do not share it.
- **Your files and server details** stay on your network and your computer.
- **Perforce:** Ropework is an independent product and is not affiliated with Perforce Software. Perforce and Helix Core are trademarks of their owner. Ropework uses the free `p4` command-line client you install yourself.
- **Keys and licence:** the beta needs no key. If you enter one, it is only recorded on your computer, and it blocks nothing.
- **Ending:** you can stop at any time and delete the app. We may end the beta or withdraw a build at any time.
- **Contact:** open an issue at https://github.com/p4inz-code/ropework/issues. A support email address will be added later.

*This notice is a draft for the beta. It will be replaced by reviewed terms before the first public release.*

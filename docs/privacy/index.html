Privacy and security

Your files and server details never leave your network.

Leaves your computer

In the beta, the app sends nothing anywhere: no telemetry, no automatic update check and no licence call. It only talks to the Perforce servers you configure.

When you ask for a beta key or write to us on this site, the answers you type in the form pass through our email service (Resend) to reach us. Nothing else on this site collects anything: no cookies, no analytics, no outside scripts or fonts.

Later, when licence checks go live: the licence id, the app version, a one-way device hash and a seat count, every few days. We will say so here first.

Never leaves your computer

Your files, file names and paths.

Server addresses, user names, changelist descriptions and logs. A feedback bundle is built on your computer, shown to you, and saved for you to send yourself.

Security

  • Passwords are never stored. They are passed once to the free p4 client and wiped from memory; the server's login ticket is kept by p4 in its own file.
  • 3D models open in a separate, sandboxed viewer that cannot reach the app, your files or the network.
  • Other programs are started only from a fixed list, with a read-only copy of the file and a time limit.
  • Every download has a SHA-256 checksum on the download page. The installers are not code-signed yet; signed installers come with the first public release.
  • Safety copies of files, kept 14 days, are encrypted with a key held by your operating system.

Your files and server details never leave your network. Ropework contacts our licence service only to confirm your licence.

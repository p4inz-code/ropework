Questions

Is it per seat?

No. One flat price per studio, by the number of people using it.

What does the beta cost?

Nothing. Beta keys are free until 1.0.

What is the Founding Studio promise?

Studios that join in the first year keep their price for 36 months.

What if the licence check cannot reach you?

After a grace period (14 days on paid plans, 10 on the free plan) the app becomes read-only. Nothing is deleted or hidden; one successful check restores everything. Beta keys never go read-only.

What about closed networks?

Every paid plan can use an offline licence file at no extra charge. Ask us; it is valid for 90 days and then renewed.

Your files and server details never leave your network. Ropework contacts our licence service only to confirm your licence.

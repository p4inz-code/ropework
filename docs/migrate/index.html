Migrate from P4V

Switch at your own pace. Nothing on your server changes.

  1. Install next to P4V

    Same server, same workspaces, nothing to convert. Use both side by side while you switch.

  2. Sign in to your server

    The first start finds the free p4 command-line client, asks for your server and user, and lists your workspaces.

  3. Learn the few differences

    Files show their state in words, a button that cannot run says why, and Get latest shows what it will change first. The tester guide walks through it.

  4. Roll out to the studio

    Today each person installs it with their own key. Shared studio profiles with layouts, templates and rules are planned.

What carries over

From P4VIn Ropework
Server, workspaces and filesUsed as they are: nothing is converted or moved
Connections and favouritesEntered once (importing them is planned)
Custom tools, bookmarks, shortcutsNot imported yet; planned for a later release
PasswordsNever stored: you sign in again, and the server's ticket is kept by p4

Your files and server details never leave your network. Ropework contacts our licence service only to confirm your licence.

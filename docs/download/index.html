The installers are not code-signed yet, so Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". Choose More info, then Run anyway, after checking the checksum below.

Check your download

In PowerShell, in the folder you saved it to:

Get-FileHash "Ropework Setup 0.18.0.exe" -Algorithm SHA256

The result must match the SHA-256 shown above.

After installing

The app works without a key during the beta; the key from your invitation goes in File, Licence. Request a beta key if you do not have one.
